Research

Entity linking maps a mention, such as a name in a sentence, to the entry it refers to in a knowledge base like Wikipedia or Wikidata. In the multimodal setting, mentions and entities come with both text and images, and the image is often what settles an ambiguous name (Figure 1).

The strongest current systems rely on large vision-language models, which are accurate but slow and costly to store for knowledge bases of millions of entities. I look for representations that keep this accuracy at a much lower cost. In FAST-MEL, mentions are encoded as compact fixed-size vectors, which makes linking orders of magnitude faster and an order of magnitude lighter in storage than state-of-the-art systems, at the same accuracy. I also work on building datasets to evaluate these methods.

Keywords: multimodal embeddings, multimodal entity linking, multimodal retrieval, vision-language models, representation learning.
